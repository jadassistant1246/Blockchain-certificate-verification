ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Protecting the authenticity of digital certificates

Our Blockchain Certificate Verification System provides a reliable way to issue, store and verify academic certificates while reducing the risk of document forgery and unauthorized modification.

Secure

Certificate information is protected using secure digital records and cryptographic hashes.

Decentralized

Blockchain technology provides a distributed record that makes unauthorized modification difficult.

Easy Verification

Certificates can be checked using their certificate ID or blockchain hash.
